# Registration

Please register using the [online registration form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdkcy57VxXeXyEYxiHpKxMEEpQEUep1f-AaUfE_rTbmVyM-gA/viewform?usp=dialog).

## Deadlines

- **November 10 (Tue), 2026** — if you wish to give a contributed talk, attend the conference dinner, or apply for travel support
- **November 24 (Tue), 2026** — attendance only (without a talk)


## Travel Support

Limited travel support may be available for students and early-career researchers who would otherwise be unable to attend and do not have access to other travel funds. 
Applicants who can use their own or their supervisor’s research funds are kindly asked to do so. 
If requests exceed the available funds, decisions and support amounts will be determined after considering individual circumstances.

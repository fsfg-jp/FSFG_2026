## Getting to the Venue

### From Fukuoka Airport
1. At Fukuoka Airport (Fukuokakuko) Station (K13) on the Fukuoka City Subway Airport Line, take a train bound for **Chikuzen-Maebaru (筑前前原)** or **Nishi-Karatsu (西唐津)**. These trains continue directly onto the JR Chikuhi Line. If your train terminates at Meinohama (姪浜), change there to a JR Chikuhi Line train.
2. Get off at **Kyudai-Gakkentoshi (九大学研都市)** Station (about 30 minutes from the airport).
3. Take a Showa Bus bound for **Kyudai Ito Campus (九大伊都キャンパス)** and get off at **Kyudai Rigakubu / School of Science (九大理学部)** (about 15 minutes). The Inamori Center is directly in front of the bus stop.

Arriving at the international terminal? Take the [free shuttle bus](https://www.fukuoka-airport.jp/en/flight/transit/index.html) to the domestic terminal (about 10 minutes), where the subway station is located.

### From Hakata or Tenjin
Hakata (K11) and Tenjin (K08) are on the same subway line. Take a train bound for Chikuzen-Maebaru or Nishi-Karatsu and follow steps 2–3 above. Nishitetsu buses also run directly from Hakata Station and Tenjin to Ito Campus (about 50 minutes).

Taxis are also available at Kyudai-Gakkentoshi Station.

- [Fukuoka City Subway Route Map](https://subway.city.fukuoka.lg.jp/eng/route/)


## Accommodation
Most hotels are located in the Tenjin and Hakata areas, which have direct train access to Kyudai-Gakkentoshi Station. Please allow about one hour from these areas to the venue, including the bus ride. A limited number of hotels are available closer to the campus, around Kyudai-Gakkentoshi and Meinohama Stations. We recommend booking early.

First Stars and First Galaxies Workshop 2026

Dates

December 8 (Tue) – 10 (Thu), 2026

Venue

Inamori Hall, Inamori Foundation Memorial Building, Ito Campus, Kyushu University, Fukuoka, Japan (Access; No. 52 on the Campus Map)

744 Motooka, Nishi-ku, Fukuoka 819-0395, Japan

About the Workshop

This annual meeting has been held for approximately two decades to review recent progress and discuss future directions in the formation and evolution of objects in the early Universe.

The topics have expanded with the development of the field and include:

  • Formation and evolution of Population III and metal-poor stars
  • First galaxies and high-redshift galaxies
  • Cosmic reionization
  • Formation and growth of massive black holes
  • High-redshift AGNs and Little Red Dots
  • High-redshift transients
  • Star-cluster formation and hub-filament systems
  • Galactic archaeology and gravitational-wave sources

Until now, this meeting has mainly served as a domestic workshop in Japan, typically attracting 50-60 in-person participants. Beginning this year, we hope to broaden participation from neighboring countries and regions and gradually develop wider international exchanges. We therefore plan to conduct talks in English wherever possible. Some student presentations may be given in Japanese.

We warmly welcome researchers and students interested in these topics, including students and early-career researchers who are new to the field.
